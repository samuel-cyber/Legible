    How it works

    1. Muse Spark reads each equation

    An AI vision model transcribes every display equation on the page into LaTeX.

    2. Checked against the PDF itself

    Legible compares that reading with the characters stored in the PDF — a deterministic check, no AI involved.

    3. Verified math, or nothing at all

    Verified equations become math a screen reader can step through. Anything unverifiable is withheld, never guessed.
